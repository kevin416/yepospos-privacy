YeposPOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

YeposPOS is a business application for authorised staff of merchants that use YEPOS services. Staff accounts are provided by the merchant or its administrator; accounts cannot be created inside the app.

Information we process

  • Sign-in and account information: merchant group number, staff account identifier, password during sign-in, authentication token, staff profile, company membership and permissions.
  • Business operations: products, customers, orders, payments, discounts, collections, pre-orders, terminal selection and related merchant records needed to operate the point of sale.
  • Cloud drive content: files and documents that an authorised user chooses to upload, together with file names, folder locations, ownership and sharing settings.
  • Device and printer settings: configured receipt-printer details and operational preferences needed to print receipts and pick slips.

Camera and Bluetooth

The camera is used for barcode, collection-code and document scanning. Barcode and collection-code images are processed on the device; the decoded value is used to find the relevant product or order. A scanned document is uploaded only when the user chooses to save it to the merchant cloud drive. A product photo is uploaded only when the user adds it to a product or uses AI photo recognition (below). Bluetooth access is used to discover and connect to compatible receipt printers.

AI photo recognition (optional)

When creating a product, a user may choose “Scan product by photo”. Before the first use the app explains this feature and asks for consent; nothing is sent unless the user agrees. With consent, the single product or box-label photo the user takes or selects is sent through the YEPOS API to OpenAI, a third-party AI provider, to read product details such as name, barcode, specification and units per box. Only that photo is sent; no account credentials, payment details or other personal data are included. The result is used only to pre-fill the product form, which the user reviews before saving. The provider does not use the photo to train its models. Consent can be withdrawn at any time in the app under Settings → General → AI photo recognition → Turn off; products can still be created by typing the details.

Customer enquiries (optional)

Where a merchant enables customer enquiries, staff with permission can read and reply to messages that the merchant’s online customers send. Messages, images and files in these conversations, staff notes, and the customer’s account details and order history that the merchant already holds are stored on and loaded from the YEPOS servers so that staff can answer the customer; they are kept for as long as the merchant keeps the conversation.

AI reply suggestions (optional)

In customer enquiries a staff member may tap to generate suggested replies or opening lines. Before the first use the app explains this feature and asks for consent; nothing is sent unless the user agrees, and nothing is sent automatically. With consent, each time the staff member taps to generate, the YEPOS API sends DeepSeek, a third-party AI provider, the most recent messages of that conversation (up to ten) and a short summary of the customer held by the merchant (recent orders, outstanding balance and frequently bought products; for opening lines, the page the customer is currently viewing on the merchant’s web shop). This content is used only to produce the suggestions shown to the staff member and is processed under DeepSeek’s API terms. A suggestion is never sent to the customer unless the staff member chooses it and sends it. Consent can be withdrawn at any time in the app under Settings → General → AI suggestions in customer support → Turn off; replies can still be typed by hand.

How information is used

We process this information to authenticate staff, enforce merchant permissions, provide point-of-sale and order workflows, synchronise merchant records, transfer files selected by users, print business documents, prevent misuse, diagnose failures and support the merchant.

Storage and service providers

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections to YEPOS-operated services and contracted infrastructure providers that host the business API and cloud-file storage. Authentication credentials are protected by the Apple Keychain. The app may keep operational caches and user-selected offline files on the device and in its private App Group container. We do not sell personal information and do not use the app for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and control

Business records are controlled by the merchant and retained according to its operational, contractual and legal requirements. Local credentials are removed when the user signs out; cached or offline business content may remain until removed in the app, removed by the merchant, or the app is deleted. Staff who need access corrected or removed should contact their merchant administrator first.

For privacy questions or requests, contact YEPOS at info@yepos.co.uk or call +44 7550 006600.

YeposPOS 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 15 日

YeposPOS 是供使用 YEPOS 服务的商户授权员工使用的业务 App。员工账号由商户或管理员提供，App 内不能注册账号。

我们处理的信息

  • 登录与账号信息：商户集团编号、员工账号、登录时输入的密码、登录令牌、员工资料、所属公司和权限。
  • 经营数据：为完成收银工作所需的商品、客户、订单、付款、折扣、取货、预订货、终端选择及相关商户记录。
  • 商户云盘内容：授权用户主动选择上传的文件与文档，以及文件名、目录、所有者和分享设置。
  • 设备与打印设置：打印收据和拣货单所需的小票打印机配置及操作偏好。

相机与蓝牙

相机用于扫描商品条码、取货码和文档。条码及取货码图像在设备上处理，识别出的编码仅用于查找对应商品或订单。扫描文档只有在用户选择保存到商户云盘时才会上传。商品照片只有在用户把它加到商品上，或使用下文的 AI 拍照识别时才会上传。蓝牙权限用于发现并连接兼容的小票打印机。

AI 拍照识别（可选）

新建商品时，用户可以选择「拍照识别产品」。首次使用前，App 会说明这项功能并征得同意；用户不同意就不会发送任何内容。用户同意后，其拍摄或选择的那一张商品或外箱标签照片会经 YEPOS API 发送给第三方 AI 服务商 OpenAI，用于读取商品名称、条码、规格、每箱数量等信息。只发送这张照片，不包含登录凭据、付款信息或其他个人数据。识别结果只用于预填商品表单，由用户核对后再保存。服务商不会用这张照片训练模型。用户可随时在 App 的「设置 → 通用 → AI 拍照识别 → 关闭」中撤回同意；撤回后仍可手动填写建档。

客户咨询（可选）

商户开启客户咨询后，有权限的员工可以阅读并回复商户网店客户发来的消息。会话中的消息、图片和文件、员工备注，以及商户本已持有的该客户账户资料和订单记录，存储在 YEPOS 服务器上并从那里读取，以便员工答复客户；保存期限与商户保留该会话的时间一致。

AI 回复建议（可选）

在客户咨询中，员工可以点按生成建议回复或开场白。首次使用前，App 会说明这项功能并征得同意；用户不同意就不会发送任何内容，也不会自动发送。用户同意后，每当员工点按生成，YEPOS API 会把该会话最近的消息（最多十条）以及商户持有的客户概况（最近订单、未结欠款、常购商品；生成开场白时还包括客户此刻在商户网店上浏览的页面）发送给第三方 AI 服务商 DeepSeek。这些内容只用于生成展示给员工的建议，并按 DeepSeek 的 API 条款处理。建议不会自动发给客户，只有员工选中并亲自发送才会发出。用户可随时在 App 的「设置 → 通用 → 客服 AI 建议 → 关闭」中撤回同意；撤回后仍可手动输入回复。

信息用途

我们使用上述信息来验证员工身份、执行商户权限、提供收银及订单流程、同步商户记录、传输用户选择的文件、打印业务单据、防止滥用、诊断故障并向商户提供支持。

存储与服务提供方

数据通过加密连接传输至 YEPOS 运营的服务及受合同约束的基础设施提供方，用于承载业务 API 和云文件存储。登录凭据由 Apple Keychain 保护。App 可能在设备及其私有 App Group 容器内保存操作缓存和用户选择的离线文件。我们不会出售个人信息，也不会把本 App 用于第三方广告或跨 App 跟踪。

保留与控制

业务记录由商户控制，并按照其经营、合同和法律要求保留。退出登录时会移除本地登录凭据；缓存或离线业务内容可能保留到用户在 App 内删除、商户删除，或 App 被卸载。员工如需更正或删除其访问权限，应先联系所属商户管理员。
