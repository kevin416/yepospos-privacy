YeposPOS Support

Help for authorised staff using the YeposPOS iPhone and iPad app.

Get help

Contact your merchant administrator for account access, company permissions, product data and printer setup. For app errors or help with the service, email info@yepos.co.uk or call +44 7550 006600.

When reporting an error, include the screen name, what you were doing, the time it happened, and the exact error message. Do not send your password.

Sign-in help

Your merchant administrator supplies your group number and staff account. If you cannot sign in, check all three fields and ask the administrator to confirm that your account is active and belongs to the correct company.

Privacy

Read the YeposPOS Privacy Policy for details about app data and account requests.

YeposPOS 支持

为使用 YeposPOS iPhone 和 iPad App 的商户授权员工提供帮助。

获取帮助

账号开通、公司权限、商品数据和打印机设置，请先联系所属商户管理员。App 报错或服务问题，请发邮件至 info@yepos.co.uk，或致电 +44 7550 006600。

反馈问题时请写明所在页面、操作步骤、发生时间和完整错误信息。请勿发送密码。

登录帮助

集团编号和员工账号由商户管理员提供。无法登录时请核对三项输入，并请管理员确认账号有效且属于正确的公司。

隐私

App 数据和账号请求说明见 YeposPOS 隐私政策。
